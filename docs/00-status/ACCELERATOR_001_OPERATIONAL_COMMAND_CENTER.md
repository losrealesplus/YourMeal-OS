# ACCELERATOR-001 · Operational Command Center (OCC)

**Status:** 🔒 **RESERVED** — strategic maximum priority · **zero implementation**  
**Declared:** 2026-08-07  
**Code:** `ACCELERATOR-001`  
**Former working name:** Universal Command Bar (⌘K) — **superseded**  
**Layer:** [Operational Accelerators](./OPERATIONAL_ACCELERATORS.md)  
**Not:** an Experience · not a Capability · not a Flow · not a module  

```text
ACCELERATOR-001
Operational Command Center

Status
Reserved

Do not open implementation.
Do not reduce to Customer search.
```

---

## Why reserve — not build — now

If we build OCC today, it becomes a **customer searcher**.

That would be a product error.

OCC must be born as the **entry point to all of YourMeal OS**:

```text
Customer · Orders · Menus · Production · Kitchen · Delivery · Billing · Settings · Everything
```

**Implementation priority:** none (now).  
**Strategic priority:** maximum.

Open only after Customer · Orders · Menus Experiences are mature — on a stable platform.

---

## What OCC is

Not a search bar.

The place where the tenant **speaks with the system**.

The operational equivalent of Spotlight (macOS) or the Command Palette (VS Code) — designed for **business operations**.

```text
Capabilities
    ↓
Experiences
    ↓
Operational Accelerators  ← OCC lives here
```

Accelerators do **not** change the domain or the Engine.  
They **accelerate work**.

## Evolution (vision only — not a sprint plan)

### Phase 1 — Find & act

```text
⌘K → Buscar cliente → Abrir → Crear pedido
```

### Phase 2 — Named party

```text
⌘K → Juan → Cliente Juan Pérez → Crear pedido
```

### Phase 3 — Quick Capture mode

```text
⌘K
Juan
2 poke
sin cebolla
lunes
    ↓
Quick Capture Mode → Preview → Crear pedido
```

Quick Capture does **not** remain a separate product.  
It becomes **OCC → Quick Capture Mode**.

### Phase 4 — Production / Kitchen

```text
⌘K → ¿Qué tengo que cocinar hoy? → Production → Kitchen
```

### Phase 5 — Delivery

```text
⌘K → Muéstrame las entregas de Adeje → Delivery
```

### Phase 6 — Billing

```text
⌘K → ¿Cuánto debe Juan? → Billing
```

At Phase 6 it is no longer a command bar.  
It is the **operating-system entry point**.

---

## Hard non-goals (until reservation lifts)

* Do **not** write OCC application code  
* Do **not** wire ⌘K / Ctrl+K in the shell  
* Do **not** scope OCC to Customer Experience only  
* Do **not** treat OCC as CX006 or as a Customer mission  
* Do **not** open Observation of OCC (nothing to observe — LAW 001-A)

---

## Lift conditions (when Reserved may become Open)

All of the following:

1. Customer Experience fluid (Create + Search mature; Edit progressing)  
2. Order Experience usable  
3. Menu Experience usable  
4. Explicit Product decision to open **ACCELERATOR-001**  
5. Design brief covers **all** operational domains above — not Customer alone  

---

## Prediction (identity)

When someone thinks of YourMeal OS years from now, they may not remember a module.

They will remember:

```text
Open the system → ⌘K → do the work
```

Protect that future. Do not ship a customer-only shortcut under this name.
