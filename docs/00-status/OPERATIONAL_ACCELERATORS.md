# Operational Accelerators

**Status:** ▶ **ACTIVE** — third product layer (Era 2)  
**Declared:** 2026-08-07  
**Laws:** PRODUCT LAW 001 · 002 · TEAM LAW 001 · EXPERIENCE MANIFESTO 001  

```text
Capabilities          — what the business is
Experiences           — how the operator works
Operational Accelerators — how work becomes faster
```

Accelerators **do not** change the domain.  
Accelerators **do not** change the Engine.  
Accelerators **accelerate work**.

---

## Stack

```text
Developer Platform     🔒 frozen
Foundation             🔒 frozen
Operational Engine     🔒 frozen (v1.0)
        ↓
Capabilities           (certified · unchanged by Accelerators)
        ↓
Experiences            (Era 2 · Return Time · measurable TTA)
        ↓
Operational Accelerators   ← this layer
```

Open Experiences until the product is **excellent**.  
Then open Accelerators until it is **extremely fast**.

## Rules

1. An Accelerator never invents a new domain Capability.  
2. An Accelerator never bypasses Facades (FOUNDATION LAW 007).  
3. An Accelerator is measured in **seconds returned**, not features shipped.  
4. **Reserved** means: vision locked · code forbidden · Customer-only scope forbidden.  
5. Opening a Reserved Accelerator requires an explicit Product decision.
