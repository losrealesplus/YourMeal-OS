## Declaration

Today, **7 August 2026**, YourMeal OS leaves the Construction era and enters the Impact era.

Era 1 built the motor.  
Era 2 returns time.

This document does not introduce software.  
It records a change of priority — permanently.

## Official product definition

> **YourMeal OS is an Operational SaaS that systematically returns time to food businesses.**

It does not define the sector.  
It defines the problem it solves.

That allows the product to grow beyond EatClean — restaurants · dark kitchens · caterings · collective dining · franchises — without renaming the mission.

---

## Permanent mission

```text
We do not build features.
We remove operational friction.
Software is only one possible solution.
Time saved is the product.
```

---

## Closing reflection · Era 1

When this project began, the conversation was React, Supabase, Capacitor, ADRs, Capabilities, and Flows.

Today the conversation that matters is:

> **How do we help a person get home earlier after their workday?**

If, in a few years, Isabella — or any other tenant — finishes the day an hour earlier because of YourMeal OS, then all of Era 1 engineering will have fulfilled exactly the purpose for which it was built.

That is the best definition of a great product.
