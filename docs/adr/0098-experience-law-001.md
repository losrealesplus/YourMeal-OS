# ADR 0098 — EXPERIENCE LAW 001 (Minimum first interaction)

## Estado

**Accepted** — 2026-08-07  
**Track:** Era 2 · Experience  
**Detalle:** [CUSTOMER_EXPERIENCE_001](../00-status/CUSTOMER_EXPERIENCE_001.md) · [EXPERIENCE_MISSIONS](../00-status/EXPERIENCE_MISSIONS.md)  
**Depends on:** PRODUCT LAW 001 · 002 · TENANT SUCCESS LAW 001 · 001-A · TEAM LAW 001

## Contexto

Formularios completos en el primer contacto miden “campos”, no trabajo. Isabella necesita empezar a operar con la información mínima imprescindible; el resto es Progressive Completion.

## Decisión

1. Accept **EXPERIENCE LAW 001**:

```text
The first interaction
must require the minimum information
needed to continue working.

Everything else
can be completed later.
```

2. Adopt **Time-to-Action (TTA)** KPIs for every Experience Mission (not CRUD field counts).  
3. Institutionalize **Progressive Completion** as the default Experience pattern.  
4. Reframe Experience epics as measurable missions (e.g. Zero Friction Customer Management · TTC &lt; 30s).  
5. Sin new Capability · sin Foundation reopen.

## Consecuencias

- First create flows ask only what unblocks the next operational step.  
- Preferences · allergies · billing · employees arrive later.  
- Experience prompts measure seconds, clicks, cognitive load — not lines of code.  
- Competing on operational speed becomes the Era 2 advantage.
